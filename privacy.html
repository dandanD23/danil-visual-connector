Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Danil Visual Connector ("the application") handles information when its owner connects a Pinterest account and uses the application to search saved boards and Pins for visual references.

Scope. Danil Visual Connector is intended as a private, read-only AI connector for the authenticated owner. It is not intended to sell personal data, run advertising campaigns, publish Pins, or provide access to other users' private Pinterest data.

1. Information the application may access

After the owner explicitly authorizes the application through Pinterest OAuth, the application may access only the information permitted by the granted Pinterest API scopes. The intended permissions are limited to data needed to read the owner's Pinterest boards and Pins.

This may include:

  • board names, identifiers and board metadata;
  • Pin identifiers, titles, descriptions, destination links and image metadata;
  • other data returned by Pinterest that is necessary to display or search the owner's saved content.

2. How information is used

The application uses authorized Pinterest data only to provide the requested functionality: searching, filtering and presenting visual references, mockups and design inspiration from the owner's saved Pinterest content.

Data is not used to create advertising profiles, sell information, or make it available to unrelated third parties.

3. Authentication and permissions

Pinterest access is granted by the owner through Pinterest's authorization flow. The application does not request the owner's Pinterest password. Access is limited to the permissions approved by the owner and may be revoked by the owner through Pinterest account settings where supported.

4. Data storage

The application is designed to minimize persistent storage of Pinterest data. Pinterest content may be processed temporarily to answer a search or retrieval request. If technical caching is introduced later, it will be limited to what is necessary for the service and handled in accordance with this policy and applicable Pinterest requirements.

5. Data sharing

The application does not sell Pinterest data. Authorized data may be processed by the technical services required to operate the connector, such as hosting or AI infrastructure, only for the purpose of providing the requested functionality and subject to their applicable security and privacy terms.

6. Data retention and deletion

The application aims to retain as little Pinterest data as practical. The owner may revoke Pinterest authorization at any time. If persistent account-specific data is stored in a future version, the owner may request deletion through the contact method below.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and authorized data. API secrets and access tokens are not intended to be exposed in public source code or public web pages.

8. Children's privacy

The application is not designed for children and is intended solely for the authenticated adult owner of the connector.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application evolves, including when Pinterest API access is approved or additional technical safeguards are introduced. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact
